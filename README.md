# Blackjack Game 🃏

A feature-rich, interactive web-based Blackjack game built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. Enjoy the thrill of classic casino Blackjack directly in your browser with added progression and achievement systems!

## 🌟 Features

- **Standard Casino Rules:** Supports standard Blackjack gameplay mechanics including Hit, Stand, Double Down, Split, and Insurance. 
- **Accurate Payouts:** Features accurate payout logic, giving you a 3:2 payout on natural Blackjacks and 1:1 on normal wins.
- **Leveling & XP System:** Earn XP by playing and winning hands to level up. Leveling up grants bragging rights and tracks your long-term dedication.
- **Win Streaks & Multipliers:** Go on a hot streak! Winning consecutive hands earns you an XP multiplier (up to 4x) and visually transforms your streak counter into a blazing fire icon.
- **Achievements:** Complete various in-game challenges like "On Fire", "Whale", and "Unstoppable" to unlock over 15 unique achievement badges.
- **Detailed Statistics:** Extensive data tracking covering your total hands played, wins/losses, blackjacks, win rate %, biggest single win, and peak balance. 
- **Persistent Progress:** All statistics, XP, achievements, and current balance are automatically saved to your local browser storage so you can easily pick up where you left off. 

## 🚀 How to Play

No installation or build steps are required. 
1. Simply clone or download the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Start placing your bets and beat the dealer!

### Game Controls
- **Deal:** Place your exact bet and distribute the cards.
- **Hit:** Draw another card. Be careful not to go over 21!
- **Stand:** Keep your current hand and pass the turn to the dealer.
- **Double Down:** Double your current bet, draw exactly one more card, and immediately stand.
- **Split:** If dealt a pair of identically valued cards, split them into two entirely separate hands playing against the dealer.
- **Insurance:** Offered if the dealer's visible card is an Ace. Pays out 2:1 if the dealer reveals a Blackjack.
- **Stats & Achievements:** View your overall progression metrics and badges at any point.
